18:T2f28,<h2 id="什么是-Monorepo">什么是 Monorepo</h2>
<p><strong>Monorepo</strong>（单一仓库）是将多个相关的项目/包放在同一个代码仓库中管理的策略。</p>
<div class="codehilite"><pre><span></span><code>my-monorepo/
├── packages/
│   ├── utils/          # 公共工具库
│   ├── client-sdk/     # 客户端 SDK
│   └── web-app/        # Web 应用
├── pyproject.toml      # workspace 根配置
└── README.md
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="Multirepo-VS-Monorepo">Multirepo VS Monorepo</h3>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th style="text-align: left;">对比维度</th>
<th style="text-align: left;">MultiRepo</th>
<th style="text-align: left;">Monorepo</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><strong>代码共享</strong></td>
<td style="text-align: left;">需要发版、发布 pip 包才能共享</td>
<td style="text-align: left;">源码级别直接引用，即时生效</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><strong>原子提交</strong></td>
<td style="text-align: left;">跨仓库改动需要多个 PR 协同</td>
<td style="text-align: left;">一次提交完成所有关联改动</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><strong>工具规范</strong></td>
<td style="text-align: left;">各仓库独立配置工具</td>
<td style="text-align: left;">统一的工具与规范</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><strong>重构成本</strong></td>
<td style="text-align: left;">跨仓库重构代价极高</td>
<td style="text-align: left;">工具链覆盖整个仓库，安全重构</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><strong>CI/CD</strong></td>
<td style="text-align: left;">多个 Pipeline 各自独立</td>
<td style="text-align: left;">统一 CI，可增量检测受影响的包</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><strong>适用场景</strong></td>
<td style="text-align: left;">团队独立、版本节奏不一致的项目</td>
<td style="text-align: left;">紧密协作的微服务、SDK 集合、工具链</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<h2 id="搭建-Workspace">搭建 Workspace</h2>
<blockquote>
<p>uv workspace文档：https://docs.astral.sh/uv/concepts/projects/workspaces/#using-workspaces</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="第一步初始化根项目">第一步：初始化根项目</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-shell"><pre><span></span><code>mkdir<span class="w"> </span>my-monorepo<span class="w"> </span><span class="o">&amp;&amp;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nb">cd</span><span class="w"> </span>my-monorepo
uv<span class="w"> </span>init
</code></pre></div>

<p>根级 <code>pyproject.toml</code>：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-toml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1"># 添加 members</span>
<span class="k">[tool.uv.workspace]</span>
<span class="n">members</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">[</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="s2">"packages/*"</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="s2">"app/*"</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="p">]</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><code>members</code> 支持 glob 模式：</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th style="text-align: left;">模式</th>
<th style="text-align: left;">匹配范围</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><code>packages/*</code></td>
<td style="text-align: left;"><code>packages/</code> 下的直接子目录</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><code>packages/**</code></td>
<td style="text-align: left;"><code>packages/</code> 下的所有子目录（含嵌套）</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><code>libs/*</code></td>
<td style="text-align: left;"><code>libs/</code> 下的直接子目录</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td style="text-align: left;"><code>apps/*</code></td>
<td style="text-align: left;"><code>apps/</code> 下的直接子目录</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>可以同时配置多个目录：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-toml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="k">[tool.uv.workspace]</span>
<span class="n">members</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">[</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="s2">"packages/*"</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="s2">"apps/*"</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="s2">"libs/*"</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="p">]</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="第二步创建成员包">第二步：创建成员包</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-shell"><pre><span></span><code>uv<span class="w"> </span>init<span class="w"> </span>--lib<span class="w"> </span>packages/agents
uv<span class="w"> </span>init<span class="w"> </span>--lib<span class="w"> </span>packages/shared
uv<span class="w"> </span>init<span class="w"> </span>app/web-service
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="第三步成员包依赖">第三步：成员包依赖</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-toml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1"># packages/agents/pyproject.toml</span>
<span class="n">dependencies</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">[</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="s2">"shared"</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="p">]</span>

<span class="k">[tool.uv.sources]</span>
<span class="n">shared</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">workspace</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kc">true</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>

<span class="c1"># app/web-service/pyproject.toml</span>
<span class="n">dependencies</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">[</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="s2">"agents"</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="p">]</span>

<span class="k">[tool.uv.sources]</span>
<span class="n">agents</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">workspace</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kc">true</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong><code>[tool.uv.sources]</code></strong> 是 UV workspace 的<strong>核心机制</strong>。它告诉 UV：<code>utils</code> 依赖不从 PyPI 下载，而是从工作区内的同名成员包中链接。</p>
<p>如果某个包在 PyPI 和 workspace 中同名，workspace 优先。需要强制走 PyPI 时可以写成 <code>utils = { workspace = false }</code>。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="可选第四步同步安装">[可选]第四步：同步安装</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-shell"><pre><span></span><code>uv<span class="w"> </span>sync<span class="w"> </span>--all-packages
</code></pre></div>

<p>执行后：</p>
<ol>
<li>UV 读取所有成员包的 <code>pyproject.toml</code></li>
<li>解析完整的依赖树（包括成员间的依赖）</li>
<li>在根目录生成统一的 <code>uv.lock</code></li>
<li>在根目录创建 <code>.venv</code>，安装所有依赖</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="第五步运行">第五步：运行</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-shell"><pre><span></span><code>uv<span class="w"> </span>run<span class="w"> </span>--package<span class="w"> </span>web-service<span class="w"> </span>python<span class="w"> </span>app/web-service/main.py
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="可选使用Makefile">[可选]使用Makefile</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-makefile"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nf">.PHONY</span><span class="o">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">run</span>-<span class="n">web</span>

<span class="nf">run-web</span><span class="o">:</span>
<span class="w">    </span>uv<span class="w"> </span>run<span class="w"> </span>--package<span class="w"> </span>web-service<span class="w"> </span>python<span class="w"> </span>app/web-service/main.py
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="可选安装第三方依赖">[可选]安装第三方依赖</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-shell"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1"># 给某个成员包添加依赖</span>
uv<span class="w"> </span>add<span class="w"> </span>--package<span class="w"> </span>&lt;成员包&gt;<span class="w"> </span>&lt;包名&gt;

<span class="c1"># 给项目根添加依赖</span>
uv<span class="w"> </span>add<span class="w"> </span>--dev<span class="w"> </span>&lt;包名&gt;
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="可选构建">[可选]构建</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-shell"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1"># 构建全部</span>
uv<span class="w"> </span>build

<span class="c1"># 构建指定包</span>
uv<span class="w"> </span>build<span class="w"> </span>--package<span class="w"> </span>utils
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="可选查看依赖关系">[可选]查看依赖关系</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-shell"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1"># 查看 workspace 中某个包的依赖树</span>
uv<span class="w"> </span>tree<span class="w"> </span>--package<span class="w"> </span>web-app

</code></pre></div>

<h2 id="包间依赖的三种模式">包间依赖的三种模式</h2>
<h3 id="源码链接workspace-模式">源码链接（workspace 模式）</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-toml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="k">[tool.uv.sources]</span>
<span class="n">utils</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">workspace</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kc">true</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<ul>
<li>本地直接引用源码，修改即时生效</li>
<li>无需 <code>pip install -e .</code> 或 <code>uv add</code> 重新安装</li>
<li>适合日常开发</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="路径引用">路径引用</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-toml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="k">[tool.uv.sources]</span>
<span class="n">utils</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">path</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s2">"../utils"</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<ul>
<li>引用工作区之外的本地包</li>
<li>路径可以是相对或绝对路径</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="Git-引用">Git 引用</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-toml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="k">[tool.uv.sources]</span>
<span class="n">utils</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">git</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s2">"https://github.com/org/utils.git"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">rev</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s2">"v0.1.0"</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<ul>
<li>引用 Git 仓库中的某个版本</li>
<li>可用于引用未发布到 PyPI 的依赖</li>
</ul>
