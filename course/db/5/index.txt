<p>事务（Transaction）是数据库管理系统中<strong>执行的一组操作</strong>，这组操作要么<strong>全部成功</strong>，要么<strong>全部失败</strong>。</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>命令</th>
<th>作用</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><code>BEGIN</code></td>
<td>开启一个事务</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>COMMIT</code></td>
<td>提交事务，使所有修改永久生效</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>ROLLBACK</code></td>
<td>回滚事务，撤销所有未提交的修改</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>SAVEPOINT</code></td>
<td>在事务内设置保存点</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>ROLLBACK TO SAVEPOINT</code></td>
<td>回滚到指定保存点，而不影响保存点之前的操作</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="k">SHOW</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">max_connections</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="k">SELECT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">pg_backend_pid</span><span class="p">();</span>

<span class="k">BEGIN</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="c1">-- 步骤1：向 B 加款 100 元</span>
<span class="k">UPDATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">SET</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">100</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">WHERE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s1">'B'</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 步骤2）：向 A 减款 100 元</span>
<span class="k">UPDATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">SET</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">-</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">100</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">WHERE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s1">'A'</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="k">COMMIT</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="c1">-- or</span>
<span class="k">ROLLBACK</span><span class="p">;</span>
</code></pre></div>

<hr />
<h2 id="ACID-特性">ACID 特性</h2>
<p>事务有四个核心特性，简称 <strong>ACID</strong>：</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>特性</th>
<th>全称</th>
<th>说明</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>A</strong></td>
<td>Atomicity（原子性）</td>
<td>事务中的所有操作要么全部成功，要么全部回滚，不存在部分执行</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>C</strong></td>
<td>Consistency（一致性）</td>
<td>事务执行前后，数据必须满足所有约束和规则</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>I</strong></td>
<td>Isolation（隔离性）</td>
<td>多个事务并发执行时，彼此之间<strong>应该</strong>互不干扰</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>D</strong></td>
<td>Durability（持久性）</td>
<td>事务一旦提交，其结果就是永久的，即使系统崩溃也不会丢失</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<hr />
<h2 id="并行问题">并行问题</h2>
<p>当多个事务并发执行时，可能出现以下三种问题：</p>
<h3 id="脏读Dirty-Read">脏读（Dirty Read）</h3>
<p>一个事务读到了另一个事务<strong>未提交</strong>的数据。</p>
<div class="codehilite"><pre><span></span><code>事务A：转账 200（未提交）
事务B：读取到余额已减少 200
事务A：回滚（余额恢复）
事务B：读到了不存在的数据！
</code></pre></div>

<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">-- 事务 A</span>
<span class="k">BEGIN</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="k">UPDATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">SET</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">800</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">WHERE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s1">'张三'</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="c1">-- 未提交</span>

<span class="c1">-- 事务 B（如果可以脏读）</span>
<span class="k">SELECT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">FROM</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">WHERE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s1">'张三'</span><span class="p">;</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">-- 800（脏数据！）</span>

<span class="c1">-- 事务 A</span>
<span class="k">ROLLBACK</span><span class="p">;</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">-- 余额恢复为 1000</span>
<span class="c1">-- 事务 B 读到的 800 是无效数据</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong>PostgreSQL 的不会出现脏读。</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="不可重复读Non-repeatable-Read">不可重复读（Non-repeatable Read）</h3>
<p>同一事务内，两次读取同一数据得到<strong>不同</strong>的结果（因为另一个事务提交了修改）。</p>
<div class="codehilite"><pre><span></span><code>事务A：第一次查询余额 = 1000
事务B：更新余额为 800 并提交
事务A：第二次查询余额 = 800（跟上一次不一样！）
</code></pre></div>

<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">-- 事务 A</span>
<span class="k">BEGIN</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="k">SELECT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">FROM</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">WHERE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s1">'张三'</span><span class="p">;</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">-- 1000</span>

<span class="c1">-- 事务 B（另一个会话）</span>
<span class="k">BEGIN</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="k">UPDATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">SET</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">800</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">WHERE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s1">'张三'</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="k">COMMIT</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 事务 A（再次查询，结果变了）</span>
<span class="k">SELECT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">FROM</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">WHERE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s1">'张三'</span><span class="p">;</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">-- 800（不可重复读）</span>
<span class="k">COMMIT</span><span class="p">;</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="幻读Phantom-Read">幻读（Phantom Read）</h3>
<p>同一事务内，两次查询同一条件的数据集，第二次<strong>多出或少了一行</strong>（另一个事务插入或删除了满足条件的行）。</p>
<div class="codehilite"><pre><span></span><code>事务A：查询余额 &gt; 500 的用户，得到 2 条
事务B：插入一个新用户，余额 600，提交
事务A：再次查询余额 &gt; 500 的用户，得到 3 条
</code></pre></div>

<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">-- 事务 A</span>
<span class="k">BEGIN</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="k">SELECT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">*</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">FROM</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">WHERE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">500</span><span class="p">;</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">-- 2 行</span>

<span class="c1">-- 事务 B（另一个会话）</span>
<span class="k">BEGIN</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="k">INSERT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">INTO</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">VALUES</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s1">'赵六'</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">600</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="k">COMMIT</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 事务 A（再次查询，多了一行）</span>
<span class="k">SELECT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">*</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">FROM</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">accounts</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">WHERE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">balance</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">500</span><span class="p">;</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">-- 3 行</span>
<span class="k">COMMIT</span><span class="p">;</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="更新丢失Lost-Update">更新丢失（Lost Update）</h3>
<p>两个事务同时读取同一数据并修改，后提交的覆盖了先提交的修改。</p>
<div class="codehilite"><pre><span></span><code>事务A：读取余额 1000，准备扣减 200
事务B：读取余额 1000，准备扣减 300
事务A：更新余额为 800 并提交
事务B：更新余额为 700 并提交（覆盖了事务A的修改！）
-- 实际应该：1000 - 200 - 300 = 500
-- 结果：700（事务A的扣减丢失了）
</code></pre></div>

<h2 id="事务隔离级别">事务隔离级别</h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>隔离级别</th>
<th style="text-align: center;">脏读</th>
<th style="text-align: center;">不可重复读</th>
<th style="text-align: center;">幻读</th>
<th style="text-align: center;">更新丢失</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><code>READ COMMITTED</code></td>
<td style="text-align: center;">❌</td>
<td style="text-align: center;">可能</td>
<td style="text-align: center;">可能</td>
<td style="text-align: center;">可能</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>REPEATABLE READ</code></td>
<td style="text-align: center;">❌</td>
<td style="text-align: center;">❌</td>
<td style="text-align: center;">❌</td>
<td style="text-align: center;">可能</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>SERIALIZABLE</code></td>
<td style="text-align: center;">❌</td>
<td style="text-align: center;">❌</td>
<td style="text-align: center;">❌</td>
<td style="text-align: center;">❌</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<blockquote>
<p>PostgreSQL 没有实现 <code>READ UNCOMMITTED</code>。如果你设置为 <code>READ UNCOMMITTED</code>，PostgreSQL 会将其当作 <code>READ COMMITTED</code> 处理。</p>
</blockquote>
<p>隔离级别越高，隔离性越好，性能越低</p>
<p>读取或设置隔离级别</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">-- 查看当前隔离级别</span>
<span class="k">SHOW</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">transaction_isolation</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 在当前事务中设置隔离级别</span>
<span class="k">BEGIN</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="k">SET</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TRANSACTION</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">ISOLATION</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">LEVEL</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">READ</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">COMMITTED</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="c1">-- ...</span>
<span class="k">COMMIT</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 也可以在事务开始前设置会话级别</span>
<span class="k">SET</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">default_transaction_isolation</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s1">'repeatable read'</span><span class="p">;</span>
</code></pre></div>

<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>应用场景</th>
<th>推荐隔离级别</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>大多数 Web 应用、报表查询</td>
<td><code>READ COMMITTED</code>（默认，够用）</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>财务系统、对账（需要同一事务内多次读取一致）</td>
<td><code>REPEATABLE READ</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>极高一致性要求（抢购、库存扣减、防超卖）</td>
<td><code>SERIALIZABLE</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>大多数情况下，使用默认的 <code>READ COMMITTED</code> 就够了。</strong> 只有在特定需求下才需要提高隔离级别。</p>
</blockquote>
