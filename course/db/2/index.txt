17:T4ee9,<h1 id="DDL">DDL</h1>
<h2 id="数据库核心概念">数据库核心概念</h2>
<p><img loading="lazy" alt="image-20260608133054687" src="https://resource.duyiedu.com/yuanjin/202606081330833.png" /></p>
<h2 id="什么是SQL">什么是SQL</h2>
<p>SQL（Structured Query Language，结构化查询语言）是操作关系型数据库的标准语言。无论使用 PostgreSQL、MySQL 还是 Oracle，SQL 的语法都高度通用。</p>
<p>SQL 主要做两件事：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>告诉数据库做什么</strong>（而非怎么做）—— SQL 是声明式语言</li>
<li><strong>操作数据</strong>—— 增、删、改、查</li>
</ol>
<hr />
<h2 id="SQL的细分">SQL的细分</h2>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>分类</th>
<th>全称</th>
<th>作用</th>
<th>常见关键字</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>DDL</strong></td>
<td>Data Definition Language</td>
<td><strong>数据定义</strong>，操作数据库和表结构</td>
<td><code>CREATE</code>、<code>ALTER</code>、<code>DROP</code>、<code>TRUNCATE</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>DML</strong></td>
<td>Data Manipulation Language</td>
<td><strong>数据操作</strong>，对表数据进行增删改查</td>
<td><code>INSERT</code>、<code>SELECT</code>、<code>UPDATE</code>、<code>DELETE</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>DCL</strong></td>
<td>Data Control Language</td>
<td><strong>数据控制</strong>，管理用户和权限</td>
<td><code>GRANT</code>、<code>REVOKE</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>TCL</strong></td>
<td>Transaction Control Language</td>
<td><strong>事务控制</strong>，管理事务</td>
<td><code>BEGIN</code>、<code>COMMIT</code>、<code>ROLLBACK</code>、<code>SAVEPOINT</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<blockquote>
<p>要操作数据，先得有存放数据的结构。所以学习顺序是 <strong>DDL → DML</strong>，先建表，再操作数据。</p>
</blockquote>
<hr />
<h2 id="DDL语言">DDL语言</h2>
<p>DDL 操作的是<strong>结构</strong>而非数据，包括：数据库的创建删除、表的创建修改删除等。</p>
<h3 id="数据库操作">数据库操作</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">-- 创建数据库</span>
<span class="k">CREATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">DATABASE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">demo</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 删除数据库</span>
<span class="k">DROP</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">DATABASE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">demo</span><span class="p">;</span>
</code></pre></div>

<hr />

<p><strong>基本语法：</strong></p>
<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="k">CREATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="err">表名</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span>

<h4 id="主键">主键</h4>
<p>主键（Primary Key）是表中<strong>唯一标识每一行数据</strong>的列或列组合。它具有两个核心特性：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>唯一性</strong>：主键的值在表中不能重复</li>
<li><strong>非空</strong>：主键的值不能为 NULL</li>
</ol>
<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">-- 单列主键</span>
<span class="k">CREATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nb">SERIAL</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">PRIMARY</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">KEY</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="p">...</span>
<span class="p">);</span>

<span class="c1">-- 复合主键（多列联合唯一标识一行）</span>
<span class="k">CREATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">enrollments</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">student_id</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nb">INT</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">course_id</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nb">INT</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">enrolled_at</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TIMESTAMP</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="k">PRIMARY</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">KEY</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">student_id</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">course_id</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="ALTER-修改表结构">ALTER — 修改表结构</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">-- 添加列</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">ADD</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">COLUMN</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">phone</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nb">VARCHAR</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="mi">20</span><span class="p">);</span>

<span class="c1">-- 删除列</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">DROP</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">COLUMN</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">phone</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 修改列的数据类型（现有数据可隐式转换时直接用）</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">COLUMN</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">score</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TYPE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nb">NUMERIC</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="mi">6</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="mi">2</span><span class="p">);</span>

<span class="c1">-- 无法隐式转换时，用 USING 指定转换规则</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">COLUMN</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">score</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TYPE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nb">INTEGER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">USING</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">score</span><span class="p">::</span><span class="nb">INTEGER</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 设置默认值</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">COLUMN</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">score</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">SET</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">DEFAULT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">0</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="mi">00</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 移除默认值</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">COLUMN</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">score</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">DROP</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">DEFAULT</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 添加 NOT NULL 约束</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">COLUMN</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">email</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">SET</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">NOT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">NULL</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 添加 CHECK 约束</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">ADD</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">CONSTRAINT</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">score_check</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">CHECK</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">score</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">&gt;=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">0</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">AND</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">score</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">&lt;=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">100</span><span class="p">);</span>

<span class="c1">-- 重命名列</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">RENAME</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">COLUMN</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">score</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TO</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">total_score</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 重命名表</span>
<span class="k">ALTER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">RENAME</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TO</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">pupils</span><span class="p">;</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong>思考：</strong> 修改列的数据类型时，如果已有数据，数据库会尝试做隐式转换。转换失败会报错。</p>
</blockquote>

<h3 id="DROP-删除表">DROP — 删除表</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">-- 删除表（连带数据一起删除）</span>
<span class="k">DROP</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 如果表存在才删除，避免报错</span>
<span class="k">DROP</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">IF</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">EXISTS</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 级联删除（连依赖该表的对象一并删除）</span>
<span class="k">DROP</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">CASCADE</span><span class="p">;</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="TRUNCATE-清空表">TRUNCATE — 清空表</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-sql"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">-- 清空表数据，但保留表结构</span>
<span class="k">TRUNCATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="c1">-- 清空所有关联表的数据</span>
<span class="k">TRUNCATE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">TABLE</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">students</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">orders</span><span class="p">;</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong>DELETE vs TRUNCATE vs DROP：</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>操作</th>
<th>类型</th>
<th>是否可回滚</th>
<th>速度</th>
<th>是否保留结构</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><code>DELETE FROM</code></td>
<td>DML</td>
<td>是</td>
<td>慢（逐行删除，可带 WHERE）</td>
<td>保留</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>TRUNCATE</code></td>
<td>DDL</td>
<td>是（PostgreSQL 中支持事务回滚）</td>
<td>快（直接重置存储）</td>
<td>保留</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>DROP</code></td>
<td>DDL</td>
<td>否</td>
<td>最快</td>
<td>不保留</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
