<h2 id="取消运行">取消运行</h2>
<p>调用 <code>client.runs.cancel()</code> 取消运行：</p>
<ul>
<li><code>action="interrupt"</code>：停止运行，但保留 Run 记录和已经产生的检查点</li>
<li><code>wait=True</code>：等到取消真正完成后再返回</li>
</ul>
<p><code>interrupt</code> 是默认的 action，这里为了讲解清楚，将它显式写出来。</p>
<div class="nb-cell"><div class="codehilite lang-nb"><pre><span></span><span class="k">await</span> <span class="n">client</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">runs</span><span class="o">.</span><span class="n">cancel</span><span class="p">(</span>
    <span class="n">thread_id</span><span class="o">=</span><span class="n">thread_id</span><span class="p">,</span>
    <span class="n">run_id</span><span class="o">=</span><span class="n">run_id</span><span class="p">,</span>
    <span class="n">action</span><span class="o">=</span><span class="s2">"interrupt"</span><span class="p">,</span> <span class="c1"># interrupt是默认值，另一个值是 rollback</span>
    <span class="n">wait</span><span class="o">=</span><span class="kc">True</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="p">)</span>
