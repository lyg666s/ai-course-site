<h2 id="Cacheable-详解">@Cacheable 详解</h2>
<h3 id="基本回顾">基本回顾</h3>
<p><code>@Cacheable</code> 标记在方法上，表示该方法的返回值可以被缓存。当方法被调用时，先检查缓存中是否存在对应的键值，如果存在则直接返回缓存值，不执行方法体；否则执行方法体，并将返回值存入缓存。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Cacheable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">key</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"#id"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Book</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getBookById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="c1">// 方法体仅在缓存未命中时执行</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="缓存名称value-cacheNames">缓存名称（value / cacheNames）</h3>
<ul>
<li>可以指定一个或多个缓存名称</li>
<li>缓存名称用于逻辑分组，不同的缓存管理器可能对应不同的存储区域</li>
</ul>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// 单个缓存名称</span>
<span class="nd">@Cacheable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">)</span>

<span class="c1">// 多个缓存名称（会同时存入多个缓存）</span>
<span class="nd">@Cacheable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"popular-books"</span><span class="p">})</span>

<span class="c1">// 使用 cacheNames 属性（与 value 等价）</span>
<span class="nd">@Cacheable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">cacheNames</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">)</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p>[!NOTE]
指定多个缓存名称时，方法执行后会同时将结果存入每个缓存中。查询时，只要任意一个缓存命中即可返回。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="缓存键key">缓存键（key）</h3>
<ul>
<li>默认使用所有方法参数组合作为缓存键（相当于 <code>key = "#root.args"</code>）</li>
<li>可以通过 SpEL 表达式自定义键</li>
</ul>

<span class="c1">// 使用参数下标</span>
<span class="nd">@Cacheable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">key</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"#root.args[0]"</span><span class="p">)</span>

</blockquote>
<h2 id="CachePut-详解">@CachePut 详解</h2>
<h3 id="作用">作用</h3>
<p><code>@CachePut</code> 总是执行方法体，并将方法返回值存入缓存。它通常用于数据更新操作，保证缓存与数据库同步。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@CachePut</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">key</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"#book.id"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Book</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">updateBook</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">Book</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">book</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="c1">// 更新数据库...</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="k">return</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">book</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="与-Cacheable-的对比">与 @Cacheable 的对比</h3>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>对比</th>
<th>@Cacheable</th>
<th>@CachePut</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>是否执行方法体</strong></td>
<td>缓存命中时不执行</td>
<td>总是执行</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>是否查询缓存</strong></td>
<td>是</td>
<td>否（直接执行）</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>是否更新缓存</strong></td>
<td>缓存未命中时更新</td>
<td>总是更新</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>适用场景</strong></td>
<td>查询方法</td>
<td>更新/新增方法</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<h3 id="核心属性_1">核心属性</h3>
<p><code>@CachePut</code> 支持与 <code>@Cacheable</code> 相同的属性：<code>value</code>、<code>key</code>、<code>condition</code>、<code>unless</code>。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// 只更新缓存，不更新缓存的情况（condition 控制）</span>
<span class="nd">@CachePut</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">key</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"#book.id"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">condition</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"#book.price &gt; 0"</span><span class="p">)</span>

<span class="c1">// 不缓存空结果</span>
<span class="nd">@CachePut</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">key</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"#book.id"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">unless</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"#result == null"</span><span class="p">)</span>
</code></pre></div>

<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Service</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="c1">// 更新图书——更新缓存</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nd">@CachePut</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">key</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"#book.id"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Book</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">updateBook</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">Book</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">book</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>

<h2 id="CacheEvict-详解">@CacheEvict 详解</h2>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="c1">// 删除数据库记录...</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="beforeInvocation-属性">beforeInvocation 属性</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>beforeInvocation = false</code>（默认）：方法执行成功后才清除缓存，如果方法抛出异常，缓存不会被清除。</li>
<li><code>beforeInvocation = true</code>：在执行方法之前清除缓存，无论方法是否执行成功。</li>
</ul>

<span class="c1">// 方法执行前清除缓存</span>
<span class="nd">@CacheEvict</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"books"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">key</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"#id"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">beforeInvocation</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kc">true</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">deleteBook</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">...</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>

<h2 id="Caching-详解">@Caching 详解</h2>
<h3 id="作用_2">作用</h3>
<p><code>@Caching</code> 用于在一个方法上组合多个缓存操作（同时使用 <code>@Cacheable</code>、<code>@CachePut</code>、<code>@CacheEvict</code>）。</p>
<h3 id="属性">属性</h3>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>属性</th>
<th>类型</th>
<th>说明</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><code>cacheable</code></td>
<td><code>@Cacheable[]</code></td>
<td>多个 <code>@Cacheable</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>put</code></td>
<td><code>@CachePut[]</code></td>
<td>多个 <code>@CachePut</code></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>evict</code></td>
<td><code>@CacheEvict[]</code></td>
<td>多个 <code>@CacheEvict</code></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

<blockquote>
<p>[!NOTE]
<code>@Caching</code> 中 <code>cacheable</code> 和 <code>put</code> 的区别：<code>@Cacheable</code> 在缓存命中时不执行方法体，<code>@CachePut</code> 始终执行方法体并更新缓存。两者组合时需要谨慎，避免逻辑冲突。</p>
</blockquote>

<h3 id="测试验证">测试验证</h3>
<p><strong>场景：新增图书后缓存更新</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>调用 <code>GET /api/books</code>（首次查询，缓存 <code>'all'</code>）</li>
<li>调用 <code>POST /api/books</code> 新增一本图书（执行 <code>createBook</code>：新增单条缓存，清除 <code>'all'</code> 缓存）</li>
<li>再次调用 <code>GET /api/books</code>（缓存被清除，重新查询数据库，重新缓存 <code>'all'</code>）</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>预期结果：</strong> 新增图书后，<code>'all'</code> 缓存被清除，保证下次查询获取最新数据。</p>
<h2 id="注意事项">注意事项</h2>
<h3 id="1-Cacheable-与-CachePut-的兼容性">1. @Cacheable 与 @CachePut 的兼容性</h3>
<ul>
<li>同一个方法上不能同时使用 <code>@Cacheable</code> 和 <code>@CachePut</code>（两者语义冲突）。如需组合，使用 <code>@Caching</code>。</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="2-缓存更新时机">2. 缓存更新时机</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>@CachePut</code> 总是在方法执行后更新缓存，无论方法是否抛出异常（除非 <code>unless</code> 条件阻止）。</li>
<li><code>@CacheEvict</code> 默认在方法执行后清除缓存，若方法抛异常，缓存不会被清除。</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="3-缓存键的冲突">3. 缓存键的冲突</h3>
<ul>
<li>确保不同的方法（尤其使用相同缓存名称时）使用不同的键，避免数据覆盖。</li>
<li>例如：<code>getBookById</code> 使用 <code>key="#id"</code>，<code>getAllBooks</code> 使用 <code>key="'all'"</code>，两者不会冲突。</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="4-方法返回值类型">4. 方法返回值类型</h3>
<ul>
<li>缓存存储的是方法的返回值，因此方法返回类型必须与缓存值类型一致。</li>
<li>使用 <code>@CachePut</code> 时，返回的对象必须是完整的，否则缓存中存储的是不完整数据。</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="小结">小结</h2>
<p>本节课我们学习了：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>CacheManager的使用方式</strong></li>
<li><strong>@Cacheable 详细用法</strong>：
   - <code>value</code>：缓存名称
   - <code>key</code>：自定义缓存键（SpEL）</li>
<li><strong>@CachePut</strong>：总是执行方法体，更新缓存，适用于数据更新/新增操作。</li>
<li><strong>@CacheEvict</strong>：
   - <code>key</code>：按指定键清除缓存
   - <code>allEntries</code>：清空整个缓存
   - <code>beforeInvocation</code>：控制清除时机</li>
<li><strong>@Caching</strong>：组合多个缓存操作，适用于复杂场景。</li>
<li><strong>实战应用</strong>：为图书管理系统设计了完整的缓存策略。</li>
</ol>
