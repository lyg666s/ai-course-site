<h2 id="回顾与导入">回顾与导入</h2>
<p>在上一讲中，我们使用 <code>TaskScheduler</code> 以<strong>编程方式</strong>提交调度任务。这种方式灵活，但代码量较大，每次都要手动创建任务、管理 <code>ScheduledFuture</code>。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// 编程式调度</span>

<span class="nd">@Scheduled</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">fixedRate</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">30000</span><span class="p">)</span>

<h2 id="Scheduled-四种调度模式">@Scheduled 四种调度模式</h2>
<h3 id="模式一fixedRate固定频率">模式一：fixedRate（固定频率）</h3>
<p><strong>含义：</strong> 以固定的频率执行任务，从<strong>任务开始时间</strong>开始计时。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Scheduled</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">fixedRate</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">5000</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">// 每 5 秒执行一次</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">fixedRateTask</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">System</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">out</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">println</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"固定频率执行: "</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">System</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">currentTimeMillis</span><span class="p">());</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>执行示意图：</strong></p>
<div class="codehilite lang-mermaid"><pre><span></span><code>gantt
    title fixedRate = 5000ms
    dateFormat ss
    axisFormat %S秒

    section 任务执行
    任务1（2s） :a1, 00, 2s
    任务2（2s） :a2, 05, 2s
    任务3（2s） :a3, 10, 2s
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>特点：</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>每隔固定时间执行一次（无论上次是否完成）</li>
<li>如果任务执行时间 &gt; 间隔时间，会导致任务堆积</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="模式二fixedDelay固定延迟">模式二：fixedDelay（固定延迟）</h3>
<p><strong>含义：</strong> 在上次任务<strong>完成后</strong>等待固定时间，再执行下一次。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Scheduled</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">fixedDelay</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">5000</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">// 上次完成后等 5 秒再执行</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">fixedDelayTask</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">System</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">out</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">println</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"固定延迟执行: "</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">System</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">currentTimeMillis</span><span class="p">());</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>执行示意图：</strong></p>
<div class="codehilite lang-mermaid"><pre><span></span><code>gantt
    title fixedDelay = 5000ms
    dateFormat ss
    axisFormat %S秒

    section 任务执行
    任务1（2s） :a1, 00, 2s
    等待5秒 :a2, 02, 5s
    任务2（2s） :a3, 07, 2s
    等待5秒 :a4, 09, 5s
    任务3（2s） :a5, 14, 2s
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>特点：</strong>
- 保证任务不会重叠执行
- 执行周期 = 任务执行时间 + 延迟时间</p>
<h3 id="模式三initialDelay初始延迟">模式三：initialDelay（初始延迟）</h3>
<p><strong>含义：</strong> 应用启动后延迟指定时间才开始第一次执行，通常与 <code>fixedRate</code> 或 <code>fixedDelay</code> 配合使用。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Scheduled</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">initialDelay</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">10000</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">fixedRate</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">5000</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">initialDelayTask</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">System</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">out</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">println</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"启动后 10 秒开始，之后每 5 秒执行一次"</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>执行示意图：</strong></p>
<div class="codehilite lang-mermaid"><pre><span></span><code>gantt
    title initialDelay = 10000ms, fixedRate = 5000ms
    dateFormat ss
    axisFormat %S秒

    section 任务执行
    应用启动 :milestone, m1, 00, 0s
    等待10秒 :a0, 00, 10s
    任务1 :a1, 10, 2s
    任务2 :a2, 15, 2s
    任务3 :a3, 20, 2s
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>特点：</strong>
- 避免应用启动时立即执行任务
- 给应用初始化留出时间</p>
<h3 id="模式四cronCron-表达式">模式四：cron（Cron 表达式）</h3>
<p><strong>含义：</strong> 使用 Cron 表达式定义复杂的调度规则（下一课详细讲解）。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Scheduled</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">cron</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"0 0 2 * * ?"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">// 每天凌晨 2 点执行</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">cronTask</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">System</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">out</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">println</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"每天凌晨 2 点执行"</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>特点：</strong>
- 最灵活，可以定义任意时间规则
- 支持秒、分、时、日、月、周等字段</p>

<h2 id="Scheduled-与-Async-组合使用">@Scheduled 与 @Async 组合使用</h2>
<h3 id="问题背景">问题背景</h3>
<p>默认情况下，<code>@Scheduled</code> 任务在调度线程池的线程中执行。如果任务执行时间长，会阻塞调度线程，影响其他定时任务。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Scheduled</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">fixedRate</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">5000</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">longTask</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">throws</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">InterruptedException</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="c1">// 这个任务耗时 10 秒</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">Thread</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">sleep</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="mi">10000</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
<span class="c1">// 由于调度线程被占用，其他定时任务可能被延迟执行</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="解决方案Scheduled-Async">解决方案：@Scheduled + @Async</h3>
<p>在 <code>@Scheduled</code> 方法上同时添加 <code>@Async</code>，让任务在独立的线程池中执行：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Scheduled</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">fixedRate</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="mi">5000</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="nd">@Async</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"taskExecutor"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w">  </span><span class="c1">// 在异步线程池中执行</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">longTask</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">throws</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">InterruptedException</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="c1">// 这个任务在独立线程池中执行，不阻塞调度线程</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">Thread</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">sleep</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="mi">10000</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p>[!NOTE]
使用 <code>@Async</code> 后，<code>@Scheduled</code> 的调度器只负责“触发”，实际执行在异步线程池中完成。</p>
</blockquote>

<h3 id="1-定时任务的异常处理">1. 定时任务的异常处理</h3>
<p><code>@Scheduled</code> 方法抛出的异常<strong>默认会被记录到日志</strong>，但不会影响后续调度。</p>

<h3 id="2-Async-与-Scheduled-的配合">2. @Async 与 @Scheduled 的配合</h3>
<ul>
<li><code>@Async</code> 方法返回类型应为 <code>void</code> 或 <code>Future</code></li>
<li><code>@Async</code> 需要 <code>@EnableAsync</code> 启用（Spring Async 系列已介绍）</li>
<li>两者组合使用时，<code>@Scheduled</code> 负责触发，<code>@Async</code> 负责执行</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="3-开发调试技巧">3. 开发调试技巧</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>缩短周期</strong>：开发时把间隔改短（如 5 秒），便于验证</li>
<li><strong>日志记录</strong>：在每个任务开始和结束时打印日志</li>
<li><strong>禁用任务</strong>：通过配置开关控制任务是否执行</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="小结">小结</h2>
<p>本节课我们学习了：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>@Scheduled 四种模式</strong>：
   - <code>fixedRate</code>：固定频率执行（从开始时间计时）
   - <code>fixedDelay</code>：固定延迟执行（从完成时间计时）
   - <code>initialDelay</code>：初始延迟
   - <code>cron</code>：Cron 表达式</li>
<li><strong>四种模式对比</strong>：任务重叠、适用场景</li>
<li><strong>@Scheduled 与 @Async 组合</strong>：避免长任务阻塞调度线程</li>
<li><strong>实战应用</strong>：为图书管理系统实现缓存预热、销量统计、缓存清理、库存同步等定时任务</li>
</ol>
