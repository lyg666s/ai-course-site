<h2 id="导入从理论到实践">导入：从理论到实践</h2>
<p>上一节课我们学习了 AOP 编程思想，理解了 JDK 动态代理和 CGLIB 代理的底层机制。Spring AOP 正是基于这些动态代理技术，提供了更简洁的、注解驱动的切面编程方式。</p>
<p>本节课我们将动手实践，将 AOP 应用到图书管理系统中，实现一个<strong>日志切面</strong>——自动记录每个接口的调用信息，包括请求参数、响应结果和异常信息。</p>
<p>首先确保在 <code>pom.xml</code> 中添加 <code>spring-boot-starter-aop</code> 依赖：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-xml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nt">&lt;dependency&gt;</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nt">&lt;groupId&gt;</span>org.springframework.boot<span class="nt">&lt;/groupId&gt;</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nt">&lt;artifactId&gt;</span>spring-boot-starter-aop<span class="nt">&lt;/artifactId&gt;</span>
<span class="nt">&lt;/dependency&gt;</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p>[!NOTE]
<code>spring-boot-starter-aop</code> 会启用 Spring AOP 的自动配置。无需额外配置即可使用。</p>
</blockquote>
<h2 id="定义日志切面">定义日志切面</h2>
<h3 id="slf4j日志类的基本操作">slf4j日志类的基本操作</h3>
<p>SLF4J（Simple Logging Facade for Java）是Spring Boot默认的日志门面，结合Logback作为底层实现。在切面类中，我们通过<code>LoggerFactory</code>获取日志实例，然后调用不同级别的方法输出日志。</p>
<h4 id="创建Logger实例">创建Logger实例</h4>
<p>每个类中通常定义一个静态的<code>Logger</code>成员变量，用于输出该类相关的日志：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="kn">import</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nn">org.slf4j.Logger</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="kn">import</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nn">org.slf4j.LoggerFactory</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">class</span> <span class="nc">LoggingAspect</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">private</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">static</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">final</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Logger</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">logger</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">LoggerFactory</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getLogger</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">LoggingAspect</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">class</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p>[!NOTE]</p>
<p>建议将<code>logger</code>声明为<code>private static final</code>，避免重复创建，且线程安全。</p>
</blockquote>
<h4 id="日志级别">日志级别</h4>
<p>SLF4J定义了5种日志级别（从低到高）：</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>级别</th>
<th>方法</th>
<th>用途</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>TRACE</td>
<td><code>logger.trace("msg")</code></td>
<td>最详细的追踪信息，通常仅调试</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>DEBUG</td>
<td><code>logger.debug("msg")</code></td>
<td>调试信息，开发环境常用</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>INFO</td>
<td><code>logger.info("msg")</code></td>
<td>关键业务流程信息</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>WARN</td>
<td><code>logger.warn("msg")</code></td>
<td>潜在问题，不影响系统运行</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>ERROR</td>
<td><code>logger.error("msg")</code></td>
<td>错误信息，需要关注和处理</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p><strong>示例：</strong></p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">trace</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"这是trace级别的日志"</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">debug</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"这是debug级别的日志"</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">info</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"这是info级别的日志"</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">warn</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"这是warn级别的日志"</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">error</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"这是error级别的日志"</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h4 id="占位符输出">占位符输出</h4>
<p>推荐使用<code>{}</code>占位符，避免字符串拼接的开销，并提高可读性：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// ❌ 不推荐（即使日志级别不满足，仍会执行字符串拼接）</span>
<span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">debug</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"用户ID："</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">userId</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"，姓名："</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">name</span><span class="p">);</span>

<span class="c1">// ✅ 推荐（占位符方式，延迟字符串构建）</span>
<span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">debug</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"用户ID：{}，姓名：{}"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">userId</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">name</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h4 id="记录异常信息">记录异常信息</h4>
<p>出现异常时需要记录异常堆栈，可将异常对象作为最后一个参数传入：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="k">try</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="c1">// ...</span>
<span class="p">}</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">catch</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">Exception</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">e</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="c1">// 输出异常堆栈</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">error</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"业务处理失败，错误信息：{}"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">e</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getMessage</span><span class="p">(),</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">e</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p>[!NOTE]</p>
<p>当传入异常对象时，SLF4J会自动打印完整的堆栈信息，便于问题定位。</p>
</blockquote>
<h4 id="配置日志级别">配置日志级别</h4>
<p>在Spring Boot配置文件中可以控制不同包的日志级别，例如：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-yaml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nt">logging</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">  </span><span class="nt">level</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nt">com.example.bookmanager</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">DEBUG</span><span class="w">          </span><span class="c1"># 整个项目的日志级别</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nt">com.example.bookmanager.aspect</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">TRACE</span><span class="w">   </span><span class="c1"># 切面类更详细</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nt">org.springframework.web</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">INFO</span>
</code></pre></div>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="p">}</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="cm">/**</span>
<span class="cm">     * 最终通知：在目标方法执行完成后执行（无论是否异常）</span>
<span class="cm">     */</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nd">@After</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"controllerMethod()"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">logAfter</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">JoinPoint</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getSignature</span><span class="p">().</span><span class="na">getName</span><span class="p">();</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">info</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"⬜ 方法执行完成: {}"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="p">}</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="代码结构解析">代码结构解析</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-mermaid"><pre><span></span><code>graph TB
    Aspect["LoggingAspect&lt;br&gt;切面类"] --&gt; Pointcut["controllerMethod()&lt;br&gt;切点定义"]
    Aspect --&gt; Before["@Before&lt;br&gt;前置通知"]
    Aspect --&gt; AfterReturning["@AfterReturning&lt;br&gt;后置通知"]
    Aspect --&gt; AfterThrowing["@AfterThrowing&lt;br&gt;异常通知"]
    Aspect --&gt; After["@After&lt;br&gt;最终通知"]

    Pointcut --&gt; Target["com.example.bookmanager.controller&lt;br&gt;包下的所有方法"]

    Before --&gt; BeforeAction["记录方法名和参数"]
    AfterReturning --&gt; AfterAction["记录返回结果"]
    AfterThrowing --&gt; AfterThrowingAction["记录异常信息"]
    After --&gt; AfterFinal["记录方法完成"]
</code></pre></div>

<p>[!NOTE]
<code>@Aspect</code> 注解标记该类为切面类，<code>@Component</code> 将其注册为 Spring Bean。两者缺一不可——没有 <code>@Aspect</code> 则不会被识别为切面，没有 <code>@Component</code> 则不会被 Spring 容器管理。</p>
</blockquote>

<h2 id="通知类型详解">通知类型详解</h2>
<h3 id="Before前置通知">@Before——前置通知</h3>
<p>在目标方法执行<strong>之前</strong>执行，常用于日志记录、权限校验、参数预处理等。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Before</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"controllerMethod()"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">logBefore</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">JoinPoint</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getSignature</span><span class="p">().</span><span class="na">getName</span><span class="p">();</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">Object</span><span class="o">[]</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">args</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getArgs</span><span class="p">();</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">info</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"🔵 调用方法: {}，参数: {}"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">args</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>执行时机：</strong></p>
<div class="codehilite"><pre><span></span><code>@Before → 目标方法 → 返回结果
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="AfterReturning后置通知">@AfterReturning——后置通知</h3>
<p>在目标方法<strong>正常返回</strong>后执行，常用于记录返回结果、缓存更新等。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@AfterReturning</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">pointcut</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"controllerMethod()"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">returning</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"result"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">logAfterReturning</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">JoinPoint</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Object</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">result</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getSignature</span><span class="p">().</span><span class="na">getName</span><span class="p">();</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">info</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"🟢 方法返回: {}，结果: {}"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">result</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>注意：</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><code>returning</code> 属性指定接收返回值的参数名，必须与方法参数名一致</li>
<li>如果目标方法抛出异常，该通知不会执行</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="AfterThrowing异常通知">@AfterThrowing——异常通知</h3>
<p>在目标方法<strong>抛出异常</strong>后执行，常用于异常日志记录、报警等。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@AfterThrowing</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">pointcut</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"controllerMethod()"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">throwing</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"exception"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">logAfterThrowing</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">JoinPoint</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Exception</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">exception</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getSignature</span><span class="p">().</span><span class="na">getName</span><span class="p">();</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">error</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"🔴 方法异常: {}，异常信息: {}"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">exception</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getMessage</span><span class="p">());</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>注意：</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><code>throwing</code> 属性指定接收异常的参数名，必须与方法参数名一致</li>
<li>可以指定异常类型，只有匹配的类型才会触发</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="After最终通知">@After——最终通知</h3>
<p>在目标方法执行<strong>完成</strong>后执行（无论是否发生异常），常用于资源清理、释放锁等。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@After</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"controllerMethod()"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">logAfter</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">JoinPoint</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">joinPoint</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getSignature</span><span class="p">().</span><span class="na">getName</span><span class="p">();</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">info</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"⬜ 方法执行完成: {}"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">methodName</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="通知执行顺序">通知执行顺序</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-mermaid"><pre><span></span><code>flowchart TB
    Start[方法调用] --&gt; Before[@Before]
    Before --&gt; Try{执行目标方法}
    Try --&gt;|正常返回| AfterReturning[@AfterReturning]
    Try --&gt;|抛出异常| AfterThrowing[@AfterThrowing]
    AfterReturning --&gt; After[@After]
    AfterThrowing --&gt; After
    After --&gt; End[结束]
</code></pre></div>

<h2 id="注意事项">注意事项</h2>
<h3 id="1-切面方法必须是-public">1. 切面方法必须是 public</h3>
<p>Spring AOP 只能拦截 <code>public</code> 方法。如果切点匹配的是非 <code>public</code> 方法，通知不会生效。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// ❌ 不会生效</span>
<span class="nd">@Before</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"controllerMethod()"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">private</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">logBefore</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">...</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>

<span class="c1">// ✅ 正确</span>
<span class="nd">@Before</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"controllerMethod()"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">logBefore</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">...</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="2-切面类自身调用无效">2. 切面类自身调用无效</h3>
<p>在切面类中调用另一个切面方法，AOP 不会拦截（因为不是通过代理调用）。</p>
<h3 id="3-避免循环引用">3. 避免循环引用</h3>
<p>切面类本身也是一个 Bean，不要注入目标对象造成循环依赖。</p>
<h3 id="4-性能考虑">4. 性能考虑</h3>
<p>日志切面会为每个匹配的方法增加额外的处理开销，在性能敏感的场景下应注意：</p>
<ul>
<li>控制日志级别（生产环境使用 INFO 或 WARN）</li>
<li>避免在日志中打印大数据对象</li>
<li>可以使用条件切点控制哪些方法需要切面</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="小结">小结</h2>
<p>本节课我们学习了：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>引入 AOP 依赖</strong>：<code>spring-boot-starter-aop</code>。</li>
<li><strong>切面类的基本结构</strong>：<code>@Aspect</code> + <code>@Component</code>。</li>
<li><strong>切入点表达式</strong>：<code>execution</code> 语法和常用写法。</li>
<li><strong>四种通知类型</strong>：
   - <code>@Before</code>：前置通知，方法执行前调用
   - <code>@AfterReturning</code>：后置通知，正常返回后调用
   - <code>@AfterThrowing</code>：异常通知，抛出异常时调用
   - <code>@After</code>：最终通知，方法完成后调用</li>
<li><strong>实战应用</strong>：为图书管理系统添加完整的请求日志切面。</li>
</ol>
