<h2 id="回顾与导入">回顾与导入</h2>
<p>在前面课程中，我们使用<code>RestTemplate</code>实现了订单服务调用图书服务：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">url</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"http://"</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">instance</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getHost</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">":"</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">instance</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getPort</span><span class="p">()</span>
<span class="w">      </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"/api/products/"</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">productId</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="n">Product</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">product</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">restTemplate</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getForObject</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">url</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Product</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">class</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>这段代码虽然能跑通，但随着调用点增多，问题会迅速暴露：</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>问题</th>
<th>说明</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>URL拼接易错</strong></td>
<td>路径参数、查询参数需要手动拼接，复杂URL容易出错</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>代码重复</strong></td>
<td>每个调用点都要写URL、指定请求方法、处理响应类型</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>可读性差</strong></td>
<td>无法从代码中直接看出“调用了哪个服务的哪个接口”</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>契约不清晰</strong></td>
<td>接口变更时，调用方没有编译期约束</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>OpenFeign的出现正是为了解决这些问题。它的核心思想是<strong>声明式调用</strong>：开发者只需要定义一个接口并加上注解，告诉框架“我要调用哪个服务的哪个接口”，剩下的服务发现、负载均衡、请求发送、响应解析全部由框架完成。</p>
<h2 id="从RestTemplate到OpenFeign">从RestTemplate到OpenFeign</h2>
<h3 id="命令式-vs-声明式">命令式 vs 声明式</h3>
<p>RestTemplate是<strong>命令式</strong>的——你需要逐步描述“怎么做”：拼接URL、指定请求方法、发送请求、获取响应。</p>
<p>OpenFeign是<strong>声明式</strong>的——你只需要描述“做什么”：调用哪个服务的哪个接口、传什么参数、返回什么类型。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// OpenFeign：声明式，只需定义接口</span>
<span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">interface</span> <span class="nc">BookClient</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/api/products/{id}"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">Product</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@PathVariable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"id"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="代码对比">代码对比</h3>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>维度</th>
<th>RestTemplate</th>
<th>OpenFeign</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>调用方式</strong></td>
<td>手动拼接URL，调用<code>getForObject</code>等方法</td>
<td>像调用本地方法一样调用接口</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>服务发现</strong></td>
<td>需要手动处理服务名或硬编码地址</td>
<td><code>name</code>指定服务名，自动从注册中心发现</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>负载均衡</strong></td>
<td>需要额外配置</td>
<td>与Spring Cloud LoadBalancer自动集成</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>代码可读性</strong></td>
<td>调用逻辑与业务逻辑混杂</td>
<td>接口即契约，语义清晰</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>维护成本</strong></td>
<td>URL变更需要修改所有调用点</td>
<td>只需修改Feign接口定义</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>关键差异：</strong> OpenFeign的<code>@FeignClient(name = "book-product")</code>中的<code>name</code>就是Nacos中的服务名，Feign会自动从注册中心获取实例列表并完成调用，<strong>调用方不需要知道服务的IP和端口</strong>。</p>
</blockquote>

<h2 id="Feign日志配置">Feign日志配置</h2>
<h3 id="为什么需要单独配置日志">为什么需要单独配置日志</h3>
<p>OpenFeign的日志输出有两个“开关”，<strong>只配置其中一个不会生效</strong>：</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>开关</th>
<th>配置位置</th>
<th>作用</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><strong>Feign的日志级别</strong></td>
<td><code>feign.client.config.*.loggerLevel</code> 或Java Config</td>
<td>决定Feign记录哪些信息</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Spring日志框架的DEBUG级别</strong></td>
<td><code>logging.level.*</code></td>
<td>决定日志能否输出到控制台</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<p>OpenFeign的日志仅在DEBUG级别下才会输出，因此必须同时设置两处。</p>
<h3 id="日志级别说明">日志级别说明</h3>
<p>OpenFeign内置了四种日志级别，通过<code>Logger.Level</code>枚举控制：</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>级别</th>
<th>记录内容</th>
<th>适用场景</th>
<th>风险</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><code>NONE</code></td>
<td>不记录任何日志（<strong>默认</strong>）</td>
<td>生产环境常规运行</td>
<td>无</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>BASIC</code></td>
<td>请求方法、URL、响应状态码、执行时间</td>
<td><strong>生产环境推荐</strong>；排查路径错误、状态码异常</td>
<td>低</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>HEADERS</code></td>
<td>BASIC + 请求头和响应头</td>
<td>排查Header传递问题（如Token是否带上）</td>
<td><strong>中</strong>：<code>Authorization</code>、Cookie等敏感头会暴露</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>FULL</code></td>
<td>HEADERS + 请求体 + 响应体 + 元数据</td>
<td>开发调试；排查参数序列化、响应反序列化问题</td>
<td><strong>高</strong>：请求体中的密码、token等敏感信息全量输出</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>生产环境原则：</strong> 常规运行使用<code>BASIC</code>或<code>NONE</code>；排查问题时临时调整为<code>HEADERS</code>或<code>FULL</code>，排查完毕后<strong>立即调回</strong>。<code>FULL</code>级别绝不可长期开启。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="方式一通过applicationyml配置推荐">方式一：通过application.yml配置（推荐）</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-yaml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nt">spring</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">  </span><span class="nt">cloud</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nt">openfeign</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">      </span><span class="nt">client</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="nt">config</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">          </span><span class="nt">default</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w">                    </span><span class="c1"># 全局配置，所有Feign客户端生效</span>
<span class="w">            </span><span class="nt">loggerLevel</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">BASIC</span>
<span class="w">          </span><span class="nt">book-product</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w">               </span><span class="c1"># 针对特定服务的配置，优先级高于default</span>
<span class="w">            </span><span class="nt">loggerLevel</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">FULL</span>

<span class="nt">logging</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">  </span><span class="nt">level</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nt">com.example.order.client</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">DEBUG</span><span class="w">   </span><span class="c1"># 必须开启Feign客户端所在包的DEBUG日志</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong>关键点：</strong> <code>logging.level.com.example.order.client</code>必须设置为<code>DEBUG</code>，否则即使<code>loggerLevel</code>配置了，日志也不会输出。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="方式二通过Java-Config配置">方式二：通过Java Config配置</h3>
<p>如果希望将日志配置作为代码的一部分，可以创建配置类：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">class</span> <span class="nc">DefaultFeignConfig</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="nd">@Bean</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">Level</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">feignLoggerLevel</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="k">return</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Logger</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">Level</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">BASIC</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="p">}</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>然后在<code>@FeignClient</code>上引用：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">configuration</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">DefaultFeignConfig</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">class</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">interface</span> <span class="nc">BookClient</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">...</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong>注意：</strong> DefaultFeignConfig类<strong>不要</strong>加<code>@Configuration</code>注解，否则会变成全局配置，影响所有Feign客户端。只有放在<code>@FeignClient(configuration = ...)</code>中或<code>@EnableFeignClients(defaultConfiguration = ...)</code>中才可控。</p>
</blockquote>

<div class="codehilite"><pre><span></span><code>[BookClient#getById] ---&gt; GET http://book-product/api/products/1 HTTP/1.1
[BookClient#getById] &lt;--- HTTP/1.1 200 (45ms)

<h2 id="Feign超时配置">Feign超时配置</h2>
<h3 id="默认超时值">默认超时值</h3>
<p>OpenFeign的默认超时时间<strong>随版本和底层客户端不同而有所差异</strong>，常见默认值为连接超时约2秒、读取超时约5秒，部分环境下也可能表现为1秒或10秒。这种不确定性本身就是生产环境的隐患——依赖默认值可能导致调用在慢响应时意外失败。</p>
<h3 id="推荐配置">推荐配置</h3>
<p>在book-order的<code>application.yml</code>中显式配置超时：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-yaml"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nt">spring</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">  </span><span class="nt">cloud</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nt">openfeign</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">      </span><span class="nt">client</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="nt">config</span><span class="p">:</span>
<span class="w">          </span><span class="nt">default</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w">                    </span><span class="c1"># 全局配置，所有Feign客户端生效</span>
<span class="w">            </span><span class="nt">connectTimeout</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">5000</span><span class="w">      </span><span class="c1"># 连接超时，单位毫秒</span>
<span class="w">            </span><span class="nt">readTimeout</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">10000</span><span class="w">        </span><span class="c1"># 读取超时，单位毫秒</span>
<span class="w">          </span><span class="nt">book-product</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w">               </span><span class="c1"># 针对特定服务的配置，优先级高于default</span>
<span class="w">            </span><span class="nt">connectTimeout</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">3000</span><span class="w">      </span><span class="c1"># 针对图书服务单独调整</span>
<span class="w">            </span><span class="nt">readTimeout</span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="l l-Scalar l-Scalar-Plain">8000</span>
</code></pre></div>

<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>参数</th>
<th>含义</th>
<th>调优建议</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td><code>connectTimeout</code></td>
<td>建立TCP连接的最大时间</td>
<td>网络稳定时可设短（如2000ms）</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><code>readTimeout</code></td>
<td>等待响应的最大时间</td>
<td>根据接口的P99耗时设置，通常为P99的2~3倍</td>
</tr>
</tbody>

<p><strong>提示：</strong> 超时配置将在后续课程中深入展开（超时与重试策略）。本节先建立“默认值不可靠、必须显式配置”的意识。</p>
</blockquote>

<h2 id="小结">小结</h2>
<p>本节课我们学习了：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>声明式调用的核心思想</strong>：OpenFeign通过接口 + 注解描述调用契约，框架负责服务发现、请求发送和响应解析。</li>
<li><strong>RestTemplate与OpenFeign的对比</strong>：命令式 vs 声明式，手动拼接URL vs 本地方法调用。</li>
<li><strong>引入OpenFeign的完整步骤</strong>：确认依赖 → <code>@EnableFeignClients</code> → 定义<code>@FeignClient</code>接口 → 注入调用。</li>
<li><strong><code>@FeignClient</code>核心属性</strong>：<code>name</code>（服务名）、<code>configuration</code>（局部配置）。</li>
<li><strong>订单调用图书的改造</strong>：将<code>RestTemplate</code>的URL拼接替换为<code>BookClient</code>的接口调用。</li>
<li><strong>Feign日志配置</strong>：<code>loggerLevel</code> + <code>logging.level</code>双开关，四种日志级别（NONE、BASIC、HEADERS、FULL）。</li>
<li><strong>超时配置</strong>：默认值不可靠，必须显式配置<code>connectTimeout</code>和<code>readTimeout</code>。</li>
<li><strong>常见问题排查</strong>：注入失败、负载均衡无实例、日志不输出。</li>
</ol>
