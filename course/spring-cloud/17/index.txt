<h2 id="回顾与导入">回顾与导入</h2>
<p>在上一节中，我们完成了<code>BookClient</code>的定义和注解改造，订单服务已经能通过Feign调用图书服务。但此时有一个问题没有解决：</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>场景：</strong> book-order需要将用户请求中的JWT Token透传给book-product，以便图书服务做权限校验。如果在每个Feign方法上都写<code>@RequestHeader("Authorization") String token</code>，一旦新增方法就很容易遗漏，而且调用方需要在每个调用点手动传入token，侵入性极强。</p>
</blockquote>
<p>拦截器可以<strong>一次配置，全局生效</strong>，所有Feign请求自动带上所需Header。本节内容详细介绍OpenFeign的拦截器机制。</p>
<h2 id="OpenFeign拦截器">OpenFeign拦截器</h2>
<h3 id="RequestInterceptor核心机制">RequestInterceptor核心机制</h3>
<p><code>RequestInterceptor</code>是OpenFeign提供的<strong>请求拦截扩展点</strong>。Spring Cloud会自动扫描容器中所有实现了该接口的Bean，在每次Feign请求发出之前调用<code>apply</code>方法，允许对即将发出的HTTP请求进行修改。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">interface</span> <span class="nc">RequestInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">apply</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">RequestTemplate</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">template</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><code>RequestTemplate</code>代表即将发送的HTTP请求，可以修改URL、请求头、请求体等。执行时机在<strong>Feign完成请求编码之后、HTTP客户端真正发出请求之前</strong>。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-mermaid"><pre><span></span><code>sequenceDiagram
    participant Client as BookClient（动态代理）
    participant Encoder as 请求编码
    participant Interceptor as RequestInterceptor
    participant HTTP as HTTP客户端
    participant Server as book-product

    Client-&gt;&gt;Encoder: 调用方法
    Encoder-&gt;&gt;Encoder: 编码为RequestTemplate
    Encoder-&gt;&gt;Interceptor: 调用apply(template)
    Interceptor-&gt;&gt;Interceptor: 注入Token/TraceId等Header
    Interceptor-&gt;&gt;HTTP: 修改后的请求
    HTTP-&gt;&gt;Server: 发送HTTP请求
    Server--&gt;&gt;HTTP: 返回响应
    HTTP--&gt;&gt;Client: 响应反序列化
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="核心场景一统一透传认证Token">核心场景一：统一透传认证Token</h3>
<p>这是拦截器最典型的用途。调用方无需在每个Feign方法上声明<code>@RequestHeader</code>，拦截器自动从当前请求上下文中提取Token并注入。</p>
<p><strong>步骤一：创建拦截器</strong></p>
<p>在book-order中创建<code>FeignAuthInterceptor</code>：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Component</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">class</span> <span class="nc">FeignAuthInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">implements</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">RequestInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">private</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">static</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">final</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">AUTH_HEADER</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"Authorization"</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="nd">@Override</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">apply</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">RequestTemplate</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">template</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="c1">// 从当前HTTP请求上下文中获取原始请求</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="n">ServletRequestAttributes</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">attributes</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span>
<span class="w">                </span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">ServletRequestAttributes</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">RequestContextHolder</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getRequestAttributes</span><span class="p">();</span>

<span class="w">        </span><span class="n">HttpServletRequest</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">request</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">attributes</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getRequest</span><span class="p">();</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">token</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">request</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getHeader</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">AUTH_HEADER</span><span class="p">);</span>

<span class="w">        </span><span class="k">if</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">StringUtils</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">hasText</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">token</span><span class="p">))</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">            </span><span class="n">template</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">header</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">AUTH_HEADER</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">token</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="p">}</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="p">}</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong>关键点：</strong> <code>RequestContextHolder</code>基于<code>ThreadLocal</code>保存当前请求信息。Feign调用通常发生在同一线程中，因此可以读取到进入book-order时的原始请求头。</p>
</blockquote>
<p><strong>步骤二：验证Token透传</strong></p>
<p>book-product中添加一个需要读取Token的测试接口：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/{id}/auth"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getByIdWithAuth</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@PathVariable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"id"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">       </span><span class="nd">@RequestHeader</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">value</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"Authorization"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">required</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kc">false</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">token</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">log</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">info</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"收到的Token: "</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">+</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">token</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="k">return</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Result</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">success</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">productService</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getBookById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">));</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>在book-order的bookClient中，定义远程调用客户端方法：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/{id}/auth"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getByIdWithAuth</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@PathVariable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"id"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>在book-order的Controller中，将用户请求的Token通过Feign调用透传：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/{id}/auth"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getProductWithAuth</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@PathVariable</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">,</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="c1">// 拦截器会自动将当前请求的Authorization头注入Feign请求</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="k">return</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">bookClient</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getByIdWithAuth</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>

<p>调用book-order的接口并携带<code>Authorization: Bearer test-token</code>，验证book-product收到的Token与传入的一致。</p>
<h3 id="核心场景二传递TraceId链路追踪铺垫">核心场景二：传递TraceId（链路追踪铺垫）</h3>
<p>在微服务调用链中，TraceId需要在服务间传递，才能将一次请求在多个服务中的日志关联起来。拦截器可以自动将当前线程MDC中的TraceId注入Feign请求头：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Component</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">class</span> <span class="nc">FeignTraceInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">implements</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">RequestInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="nd">@Override</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">void</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">apply</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">RequestTemplate</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">template</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">traceId</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">MDC</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">get</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"traceId"</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="k">if</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">StringUtils</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">hasText</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">traceId</span><span class="p">))</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">            </span><span class="n">template</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">header</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"X-Trace-Id"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">traceId</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="p">}</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="p">}</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong>课程衔接：</strong>后续课程的可观测性部分将使用Micrometer Tracing自动完成TraceId的跨服务传播，届时拦截器不再是必须的。但在尚未引入链路追踪框架的阶段，拦截器是手动传递TraceId的轻量方案。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="拦截器的配置方式全局-vs-局部">拦截器的配置方式：全局 vs 局部</h3>
<p>拦截器有两种生效方式，理解它们的区别是避免“配置污染”的关键。</p>
<p><strong>方式一：全局拦截器（@Component / @Bean）</strong></p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Component</span><span class="w">   </span><span class="c1">// 注册为Spring Bean，对所有Feign客户端生效</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">class</span> <span class="nc">FeignAuthInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">implements</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">RequestInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">...</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>只要拦截器被注册为Spring容器中的Bean，Spring Cloud就会将它注入到<strong>每一个</strong>Feign客户端中。</p>
<p><strong>方式二：局部拦截器（通过@FeignClient的configuration）</strong></p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// 配置类：不加 @Configuration</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">class</span> <span class="nc">BookFeignConfig</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nd">@Bean</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">RequestInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">bookAuthInterceptor</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="k">return</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="k">new</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">BookAuthInterceptor</span><span class="p">();</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="p">}</span>
<span class="p">}</span>

<span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">configuration</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">BookFeignConfig</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">class</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">interface</span> <span class="nc">BookClient</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">...</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>局部拦截器只对声明了<code>configuration</code>的那个Feign客户端生效。</p>
<h3 id="拦截器执行顺序">拦截器执行顺序</h3>
<p>当存在多个拦截器时，可以通过<code>@Order</code>或实现<code>Ordered</code>接口控制执行顺序。<code>AnnotationAwareOrderComparator.sort(interceptors)</code> 会对拦截器列表排序，<code>@Order</code>值越小越先执行。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Component</span>
<span class="nd">@Order</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="mi">1</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w">   </span><span class="c1">// 先执行：注入TraceId</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">class</span> <span class="nc">FeignTraceInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">implements</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">RequestInterceptor</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">...</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>

<h2 id="小结">小结</h2>
<p>本节课我们学习了：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong><code>RequestInterceptor</code>核心机制</strong>：在Feign请求发出前调用<code>apply(RequestTemplate template)</code>，可修改Header、URL等。</li>
<li><strong>拦截器核心场景</strong>：统一透传Token、传递TraceId，实现“一次配置，全局生效”。</li>
<li><strong>全局 vs 局部拦截器</strong>：<code>@Component</code>注册为全局；不加<code>@Configuration</code>的配置类 + <code>@FeignClient(configuration = ...)</code>实现局部。<code>@Configuration</code>会导致局部配置“污染”全局。</li>
<li><strong>拦截器执行顺序</strong>：通过<code>@Order</code>控制，TraceId类拦截器优先执行。</li>
<li><strong>实战整合</strong>：<code>BookClient</code>配置BASIC日志 + 全局TraceId/Token拦截器，验证日志输出和Token透传效果。</li>
</ol>
