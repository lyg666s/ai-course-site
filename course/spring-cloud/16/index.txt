<h2 id="FeignClient属性详解">@FeignClient属性详解</h2>
<h3 id="name-value服务名必填">name / value：服务名（必填）</h3>
<p><code>name</code>（或<code>value</code>）是<code>@FeignClient</code>最核心的属性，用于指定要调用的<strong>服务名</strong>。Spring Cloud会基于这个名称创建对应的LoadBalancer客户端。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">)</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>从Spring Cloud OpenFeign的某个版本开始，<code>name</code>已成为<strong>必需属性</strong>，即使配置了<code>url</code>也不能省略。<code>name</code>支持占位符，可以从配置文件中读取：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"${feign.book-product.name:book-product}"</span><span class="p">)</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong>注意：</strong> <code>name</code>的值必须与book-product的<code>spring.application.name</code>完全一致，区分大小写。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="path统一路径前缀">path：统一路径前缀</h3>
<p>如果book-product的所有接口都有一个统一前缀（如<code>/api</code>），可以在<code>@FeignClient</code>上通过<code>path</code>声明，避免在每个方法上重复写：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">path</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"/api"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">interface</span> <span class="nc">BookClient</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/{id}"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w">          </span><span class="c1">// 实际请求：/api/products/{id}</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@PathVariable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"id"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<blockquote>
<p><strong>适用场景：</strong> 当Controller类上有<code>@RequestMapping("/api/products")</code>时，Feign接口可以用<code>path</code>声明<code>/api</code>，方法上只写<code>/products/{id}</code>。但需要注意，如果Controller的路径分散在多个类中（如<code>/api/products</code>、<code>/api/stock</code>），<code>path</code>只能声明公共部分，不能覆盖所有场景。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="url绕过服务发现仅调试">url：绕过服务发现（仅调试）</h3>
<p><code>url</code>属性用于直接指定目标地址，<strong>配置后将绕过服务发现和负载均衡</strong>，直接向该地址发送请求：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">url</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"http://localhost:8081"</span><span class="p">)</span>
</code></pre></div>

<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>场景</th>
<th>是否使用<code>url</code></th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>生产环境通过Nacos发现服务</td>
<td><strong>不使用</strong></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>本地调试，提供方未注册到Nacos</td>
<td>可以使用</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>对接外部第三方API（无注册中心）</td>
<td>可以使用</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>生产环境警告：</strong> 一旦配置<code>url</code>，Feign将不再从Nacos获取实例列表，负载均衡和故障转移能力全部失效。<strong>生产环境不要配置<code>url</code></strong>。</p>
</blockquote>
<h3 id="configuration局部配置类">configuration：局部配置类</h3>
<p><code>configuration</code>用于为单个Feign客户端指定自定义配置类，覆盖默认的<code>FeignClientsConfiguration</code>中的Bean（如<code>Decoder</code>、<code>Encoder</code>、<code>Contract</code>、<code>Logger.Level</code>等）。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">configuration</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">BookFeignConfig</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">class</span><span class="p">)</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="contextId解决同名客户端的Bean冲突">contextId：解决同名客户端的Bean冲突</h3>
<p>当需要为<strong>同一个服务</strong>创建多个Feign客户端，且每个客户端使用不同的自定义配置时，直接写两个<code>name = "book-product"</code>的接口会导致配置Bean名称冲突。此时通过<code>contextId</code>为每个客户端指定唯一的上下文标识：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">contextId</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"bookQueryClient"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">configuration</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">QueryConfig</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">class</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">interface</span> <span class="nc">BookQueryClient</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">...</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>

<span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">contextId</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"bookStockClient"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">configuration</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">StockConfig</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">class</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">interface</span> <span class="nc">BookStockClient</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">...</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><code>contextId</code>会作为该客户端配置Bean名称的一部分，避免冲突。如果只有一个客户端，不需要设置<code>contextId</code>。</p>
<h3 id="qualifiers自定义Bean别名">qualifiers：自定义Bean别名</h3>
<p>默认情况下，Feign客户端Bean的名称是接口的全限定名。如果希望按自定义名称注入，可以使用<code>qualifiers</code>：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">qualifiers</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"bookClientV1"</span><span class="p">)</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>然后在注入时配合<code>@Qualifier("bookClientV1")</code>使用。日常开发中较少使用，了解即可。</p>

<h2 id="方法级注解与参数绑定">方法级注解与参数绑定</h2>
<h3 id="HTTP方法注解">HTTP方法注解</h3>
<p>Feign接口方法上使用Spring MVC的HTTP注解声明请求类型，与Controller的写法完全一致：</p>
<h3 id="PathVariable路径参数">@PathVariable：路径参数</h3>
<p>与Controller的<code>@PathVariable</code>用法一致，用于填充URL模板中的占位符：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/{id}"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@PathVariable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"id"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>坑点：</strong> <code>@PathVariable</code>的<code>value</code>必须显式指定，且与URL模板中的占位符名称一致。如果省略<code>value</code>，依赖编译时的参数名保留（<code>-parameters</code>编译选项），否则可能绑定失败。</p>
<h3 id="RequestParam查询参数">@RequestParam：查询参数</h3>
<p>用于拼接在URL后面的查询参数：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/search"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">List</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">search</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@RequestParam</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"keyword"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">keyword</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">                                </span><span class="nd">@RequestParam</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"page"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">int</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">page</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">                                </span><span class="nd">@RequestParam</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"size"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kt">int</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">size</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>高频坑点：</strong> <code>@RequestParam</code>的<code>value</code><strong>必须显式指定</strong>。如果参数名与形参名不一致，或者编译时未保留参数名，省略<code>value</code>会导致<strong>参数静默丢失</strong>，请求正常发出但提供方收不到参数。</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// 错误写法：可能丢失参数</span>
<span class="nd">@RequestParam</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">keyword</span>

<span class="c1">// 正确写法：显式指定 value</span>
<span class="nd">@RequestParam</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"keyword"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">keyword</span>
</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="RequestHeader请求头传递">@RequestHeader：请求头传递</h3>
<p>用于传递认证Token、租户ID等请求头信息：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/{id}"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@PathVariable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"id"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">,</span>
<span class="w">                           </span><span class="nd">@RequestHeader</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"Authorization"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">String</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">token</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><code>@RequestHeader</code>同样建议显式指定<code>value</code>。如果需要在<strong>所有方法</strong>中自动传递某个请求头（如Token），手动在每个方法上加<code>@RequestHeader</code>会产生大量重复。后续课程将通过<strong>Feign拦截器</strong>统一处理这种场景。</p>
<h3 id="RequestBody请求体POSTPUT">@RequestBody：请求体（POST/PUT）</h3>
<p>用于传递JSON请求体，适用于POST、PUT等有请求体的方法：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@PostMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/stock/deduct"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">Void</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">deductStock</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@RequestBody</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">StockDeductRequest</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">request</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>注意事项：</strong></p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>注意点</th>
<th>说明</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td>GET请求不应使用<code>@RequestBody</code></td>
<td>部分HTTP客户端和服务器不支持GET携带请求体，Feign可能报错或行为异常</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>一个方法最多一个<code>@RequestBody</code></td>
<td>HTTP请求体只能有一个</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>请求体对象需要无参构造函数</td>
<td>序列化和反序列化依赖Jackson，缺少无参构造会导致失败</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
<h3 id="SpringQueryMapGET请求传递对象参数">@SpringQueryMap：GET请求传递对象参数</h3>
<p>这是OpenFeign中<strong>最容易踩坑</strong>的场景。假设提供方有一个GET接口，参数是一个对象：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// 提供方（book-product）的接口</span>
<span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/query"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">List</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">query</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">ProductQuery</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">query</span><span class="p">);</span><span class="w">   </span><span class="c1">// ProductQuery 包含 keyword、category、minPrice 等字段</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>在Feign接口中，如果直接写<code>@GetMapping(...) Result&lt;List&lt;ProductDto&gt;&gt; query(ProductQuery query)</code>，Feign无法将对象的字段自动展开为查询参数，可能导致参数丢失或被错误处理。<strong>正确做法是使用<code>@SpringQueryMap</code></strong>，它会将POJO或Map的字段自动映射为查询参数：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// 调用方（book-order）的Feign接口</span>
<span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/query"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">List</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">query</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@SpringQueryMap</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">ProductQuery</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">query</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><code>@SpringQueryMap</code>是Spring Cloud OpenFeign对原生<code>@QueryMap</code>的封装，专门用于解决GET请求传递对象参数的场景。</p>

<h2 id="实战扩展BookClient覆盖多种参数场景">实战：扩展BookClient覆盖多种参数场景</h2>
<h3 id="完整BookClient接口">完整BookClient接口</h3>
<p>将上一节的<code>BookClient</code>改造为使用DTO并覆盖多种参数类型：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@FeignClient</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">name</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"book-product"</span><span class="p">,</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">path</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="s">"/api"</span><span class="p">)</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="cm">/** 按条件查询图书（@SpringQueryMap） */</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="cm">/** 扣减库存（@RequestBody） */</span>

</code></pre></div>

<h3 id="场景一按ID查询图书PathVariable-ProductDto">场景一：按ID查询图书（@PathVariable + ProductDto）</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/{id}"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@PathVariable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"id"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>Service中调用：</p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@Service</span>
<span class="nd">@RequiredArgsConstructor</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">class</span> <span class="nc">BookQueryService</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">private</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">final</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">BookClient</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">bookClient</span><span class="p">;</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getProductById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">productId</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">result</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">bookClient</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="n">productId</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="w">        </span><span class="k">return</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">result</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">!=</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kc">null</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="o">?</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">result</span><span class="p">.</span><span class="na">getData</span><span class="p">()</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">:</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kc">null</span><span class="p">;</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="p">}</span>
<span class="p">}</span>

<p>调用方拿到的是<strong>类型安全的<code>ProductDto</code></strong>，可以直接访问<code>product.getTitle()</code>、<code>product.getPrice()</code>等字段，无需强转或从Map中取值。</p>
<h3 id="场景二按条件查询图书SpringQueryMap-ProductQuery">场景二：按条件查询图书（@SpringQueryMap + ProductQuery）</h3>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/query"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">List</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">query</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@SpringQueryMap</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">ProductQuery</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">query</span><span class="p">);</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p>Service中调用：</p>

<h2 id="Feign接口继承特性">Feign接口继承特性</h2>
<h3 id="为什么需要继承">为什么需要继承</h3>
<p>在book-order中定义<code>BookClient</code>时，路径、参数注解都是从book-product的Controller"抄"过来的。如果book-product修改了接口路径，book-order的<code>BookClient</code>必须同步修改，否则运行时才会报错。<strong>接口继承特性</strong>允许将API定义抽取到一个共享的接口中，提供方实现该接口，调用方继承该接口，从而保证契约的一致性。</p>
<h3 id="改造步骤">改造步骤</h3>
<p><strong>步骤一：在book-common中定义API接口</strong></p>
<div class="codehilite lang-java"><pre><span></span><code><span class="c1">// book-common</span>
<span class="kd">public</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="kd">interface</span> <span class="nc">BookApi</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="p">{</span>

<span class="w">    </span><span class="nd">@GetMapping</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"/products/{id}"</span><span class="p">)</span>
<span class="w">    </span><span class="n">Result</span><span class="o">&lt;</span><span class="n">ProductDto</span><span class="o">&gt;</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="nf">getById</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="nd">@PathVariable</span><span class="p">(</span><span class="s">"id"</span><span class="p">)</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">Long</span><span class="w"> </span><span class="n">id</span><span class="p">);</span>
<span class="p">}</span>
</code></pre></div>

<p><strong>步骤二：提供方（book-product）实现该接口</strong></p>

<h2 id="小结">小结</h2>
<p>本节课我们学习了：</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>DTO的定义与使用</strong>：在book-common中定义<code>ProductDto</code>、<code>ProductQuery</code>、<code>StockDeductRequest</code>，替代<code>Object</code>，实现类型安全。</li>
<li><strong><code>@FeignClient</code>核心属性</strong>：<code>name</code>（必需，服务名）、<code>path</code>（统一前缀）、<code>url</code>（仅调试，绕过服务发现）、<code>configuration</code>（局部配置，不加<code>@Configuration</code>）、<code>contextId</code>（解决同名冲突）、<code>qualifiers</code>（自定义别名）。</li>
<li><strong>HTTP方法注解</strong>：<code>@GetMapping</code>、<code>@PostMapping</code>等，与方法一一对应，不能省略。</li>
<li><strong>参数绑定注解</strong>：<code>@PathVariable</code>（路径）、<code>@RequestParam</code>（查询，必须写<code>value</code>）、<code>@RequestHeader</code>（请求头）、<code>@RequestBody</code>（请求体）、<code>@SpringQueryMap</code>（GET对象参数）。</li>
<li><strong>高频坑点</strong>：<code>@RequestParam</code>省略<code>value</code>导致参数静默丢失；GET请求对象参数未用<code>@SpringQueryMap</code>；缺少HTTP注解导致GET被改写为POST。</li>
<li><strong>Feign接口继承特性</strong>：在共享模块中定义API接口，提供方实现、调用方继承，保证契约一致、减少重复定义。</li>
<li><strong>实战改造</strong>：将<code>BookClient</code>从单一方法扩展为覆盖路径参数、查询参数、请求体、对象查询的完整客户端，并使用DTO替换<code>Object</code>。</li>
</ol>
